What should summaries include?

A web app cannot wake your phone while it is closed. Summaries appear when the app is open at the set time, or the next time you open it after that time. Budget alerts appear as soon as you add an entry.

About Cedi Tracker Test

Cedi Tracker is a simple personal finance app for tracking money received and spent, monthly budgets, savings, investments and debts. Version 3.0.

What it does

Daily, weekly and monthly totals, spending by category, budget alerts, linked savings, investment and debt records, Excel and text statements, up to 3 profiles, a PIN lock and daily summaries.

Your data

Everything is stored only on this phone, in this browser. Nothing is sent to a server. Download a backup regularly from Settings.
